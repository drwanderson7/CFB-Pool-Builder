How to use this tool

Builds two 7-pick Splash entries that share exactly the number of picks you choose, favoring the teams you feel best about.

Quick start

  1. Add teams. In section 1, paste this week's candidates, one per line, with the spread after the name (for example Purdue +14.5). Tap Load / update teams.
  2. Set the overlap. In section 2, choose how many picks must appear in both entries (the Shared menu).
  3. Set each team's tier. In section 3, use the Tier menu: Lock, Strong, Lean, or Meh. Every team starts at Lean.
  4. Optional: fine-tune. Use Min and Max to limit how many entries a team can be in. Use S1, S2, or the lock to force a team into Entry 1, Entry 2, or both. On a phone these settings are tucked behind each team's Edit button.
  5. Build. Tap Build 2 entries. Tap Another valid build for a different arrangement.
  6. Adjust. On any pick, Move to sends it to the other entry and Unlock releases a locked pick. Undo reverses your last change.
  7. Submit and record. Tap Copy entry under each entry to copy its list, then tap Save this week.
  8. Grade it. After the games, open Saved weeks and tap the dash beside each pick to mark it W, L, or Push. For weeks you didn't save here, tap Add a past week and enter the records by hand.
  9. Next week. Tap Start new week in section 1. Your overlap setting and saved weeks are kept.

What you get

  • Two entries of 7 picks. Shared picks are listed first, highlighted amber with a Shared badge.
  • Each pick shows its spread as a chip: dark for favorites, light for underdogs. Each entry header shows the mix, like "5 dogs · 2 favs".
  • A Benched list under the entries with every team left out of both, color-coded by tier.
  • Copy entry gives a numbered list in on-screen order, using your original team names.
  • Saved weeks keeps each build you save, with your record per entry, your season record, and your record by tier.

Key features

Tiers
Lock teams get the shared slots first, then Strong, then Lean. Meh teams are benched first. Teams in the same tier are treated as equally good and shuffled each build, so rebuilding only varies teams you feel the same about. If the pool needs a lower tier to fill the entries, it's used.
Order within a tier
The ▲▼ arrows (or dragging on desktop) reorder teams within their own tier. That order keeps your build stable when you make small edits. To change a team's tier, use its Tier menu.
Min and Max
How many entries a team may be in (0, 1, or 2). Max 0 benches a team. Min 2 puts it in both entries.
Place (S1, S2, lock)
S1 or S2 locks a team into that entry. The lock puts it in both, which counts toward your shared picks.
Shared picks (overlap)
The number of teams that appear in both entries. You need at least 14 minus that many teams. If you don't have enough, the message in the build bar offers a button to fix it.
Undo
The Undo button or Ctrl/Cmd+Z reverses your last 30 changes. It resets when the page reloads or when changes arrive from your other device. Saving and grading weeks aren't part of Undo.
Past weeks
In Saved weeks, Add a past week lets you enter a week's records without the individual picks: Entry 1, Entry 2, your 2/2 picks (in both entries), and your 1/2 picks (in just one). Entry 1 + Entry 2 always equals 2 × (2/2 picks) + (1/2 picks), so the 1/2 row fills itself in and you're warned if the numbers don't add up. These weeks count toward your season record.
Sync
The pill at the top shows whether your laptop and phone are in step. Tap it to sync now.
Copy / Load setup
Makes a backup code (or file) of your current setup that you can load on another device.

Common questions

Build 2 entries is greyed out.

Read the message in the build bar; it says what's wrong. The most common cause is too few teams for your overlap. If so, tap the button in the message (for example "Set overlap to 4"). You need at least 7 teams with Max above 0.

My Lock team isn't in both entries.

Only as many teams can be in both entries as your shared-picks number. With 3 shared picks, extra Lock teams land in a single entry. Raise the overlap if you want more teams in both.

A Meh team got picked.

Two 7-pick entries need a certain number of different teams. When your higher tiers can't cover them, the builder reaches down into lower tiers.

Another valid build gave me different teams.

That's expected. Teams in the same tier are shuffled each time. If you want a team favored, move it to a higher tier.

I can't drag a team into another tier.

Tiers change only through the Tier menu. Dragging and the arrows reorder teams within their own tier.

Dragging doesn't work on my phone.

Touch screens don't support dragging here. Use the ▲▼ arrows, the Move to buttons, and S1/S2/lock instead.

A team's spread doesn't show as a chip.

The line needs a space and a sign after the name, like Purdue +14.5, Rice -3, or Kansas St PK.

My other device isn't showing my changes.

Check the sync pill at the top. "Synced" means you're up to date; tap it to fetch the latest. "Offline" means changes are saved on this device and will sync when it's back online. "Sync not set up" means the sync route isn't connected on your site, so changes stay on this device.

I made a mistake.

Tap Undo (or press Ctrl/Cmd+Z). If you tapped Start new week by accident, Undo brings back your teams and build.

How do I add weeks from before I started using the tool?

Open Saved weeks and tap Add a past week. Enter the week number and each record as wins, losses, and pushes. Use Edit on a saved week to fix a typo. By-tier records only include builds you saved here, since tiers aren't known for weeks entered by hand.

Can I use it with a keyboard?

Yes. Tab moves between controls, Enter or Space presses a button, and Escape closes any pop-up. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes your last change. While a pop-up is open, Tab stays inside it.

The team list is very long on my phone.

Each team shows as one line. Tap Edit on a team to open its Min, Max, and Place settings, or tap Expand all above the list to open every team at once.

I pasted more than 40 teams.

The list holds up to 40 teams. Anything after the 40th is ignored.
